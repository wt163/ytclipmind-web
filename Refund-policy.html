Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains the refund terms for purchases made through YT ClipMind.

1. General Refund Policy

Due to the digital nature of YT ClipMind services, payments are generally non-refundable unless required by applicable law.

2. Subscription Cancellation

Users may cancel automatic renewal at any time.

After cancellation:

  • Access remains available until the end of the current billing period.
  • Future renewal charges will not occur.

3. Lifetime Purchases

Lifetime purchases provide access to available Pro features without recurring payments.

Lifetime purchases are generally non-refundable unless required by applicable law.

4. Payment Processing

Payments are processed through Paddle.

Refund requests may be subject to Paddle's payment policies and applicable regulations.

5. Contact

For refund-related questions: support@ytclipmind.com
